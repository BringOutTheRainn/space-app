# Stardust Empire privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Stardust Empire is an idle game for Android. This policy explains what information the game and the
services inside it collect, and why.

## What the game itself stores

- **Your progress** (Stardust, generators, upgrades, settings and which in-game items you bought) is
  saved only on your phone. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes
  it. You can copy your progress out with Settings, Copy save code.
- **Reminders** are optional. If you turn them on, the game schedules notifications on your phone only.
  No push server is involved.
- **Error reports**: if the game hits an unexpected error, a short technical note (the error and your
  phone's browser version) is kept on your phone for up to seven days. It is only shared if you copy
  it (from Settings, or from the screen shown if the game cannot start) and send it yourself.
- The game has **no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics** of its own.

## Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. Most ads are optional: you choose to watch them for a
reward. AdMob may collect:

- your device's advertising ID,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- information about how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic data.

Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area,
the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing
personalized ads, and you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising
ID in your phone's Google settings.

How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game keeps
a record on your phone of which products you own so it can unlock them, and Google Play confirms
purchases when you restore them.

## Children

Stardust Empire is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from them.

## Data sharing and security

We do not sell or share your information. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google AdMob
and Google Play collect as described above, sent encrypted over HTTPS.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. For questions,
contact the developer at the email address shown on the game's Google Play store page.
